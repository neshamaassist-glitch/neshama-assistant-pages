Neshama Personal Assistant

Privacy policy

Neshama Personal Assistant is a privately operated, AI-assisted productivity tool for its operator and personally known household users. This policy explains its handling of Google user data.

Information accessed

The application accesses Google account information and service data only through the permissions granted to a particular connection. These can include Gmail account identity, message metadata, message contents and attachments; calendar events; Drive files; contacts; and Docs and Sheets content. Access depends on the authorized connection and task. The separate lesson-delivery connection has Gmail reading and sending permissions, not access to Calendar, Drive, Contacts, Docs, or Sheets.

Task instructions, assistant conversations, downloaded files, and operational records may also be stored to carry out the operator's requests and maintain continuity.

How information is used

Information is used to provide requested personal-assistant features, such as finding and summarizing relevant information, drafting or sending authorized messages, delivering requested recordings, organizing documents and events, and diagnosing operational failures. Sending a message or changing external data remains subject to the applicable user authorization; an OAuth grant alone is not general permission to take every available action.

This application does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to develop or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

AI processing and other disclosures

When needed for an authorized task, selected Google data and task context may be transmitted to the AI model provider configured by the operator. This processing is separate from Google's APIs. The provider used can vary with the configuration; its own processing, retention, and security terms also apply. Do not assume all assistant processing stays on the operator's computer.

Messages, attachments, or documents may be sent to recipients or services specifically authorized for a task. The operator and authorized household users may see information relevant to their authorized tasks; the application is not a public multi-user service.

The public information pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy statement. These pages do not collect Google credentials, provide a Google sign-in form, or include application-added analytics or advertising scripts.

Storage, security, and retention

Google OAuth credentials are stored separately from these public pages on the operator-controlled computer. Downloaded data, conversations, delivery receipts, logs, and backups may remain on that computer to support requested work, diagnosis, and duplicate-send prevention. There is no single automatic deletion period for all of these records.

Credentials, private messages, and documents are not intended to be included in the public website repository. Access controls and authorization checks are used, but no system can guarantee absolute security. Storage and retention by Google, AI providers, or authorized destination services are governed by their respective policies and settings.

Revoking access and requesting deletion

You can review and revoke the application's Google access from Google Account connections. Revocation prevents future access through the revoked grant; it does not automatically remove files, messages, conversations, or backups already retained.

For help reviewing or deleting retained application data, contact neshama.assist@gmail.com. Requests must be verified and reviewed for the relevant records and services. Previously delivered email and copies held by recipients or other services are outside the application's direct control.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Neshama Personal Assistant's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the assistant's configuration or data practices. The public repository's revision history records changes to this page.

Support and privacy contact: neshama.assist@gmail.com.
