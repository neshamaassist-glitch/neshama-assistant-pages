Neshama Personal Assistant

A privately operated assistant for managing authorized email and everyday productivity tasks.

What the assistant does

Neshama Personal Assistant uses Hermes Agent software on an operator-controlled computer. It helps its operator and personally known household users with tasks such as finding relevant emails, drafting messages, delivering requested lesson recordings, and organizing information.

Depending on the account's granted permissions and the requested task, Google integrations can access Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Contacts, Docs, and Sheets. Not every account grants every permission. The lesson-delivery connection uses Gmail reading and sending permissions only.

Access and authorization

Google access is granted through Google's OAuth consent process. The assistant should act only within the operator's instructions and the permissions granted to the particular connection. Sending messages, sharing information, or changing external data requires the applicable user authorization.

This is an informational website, not a public assistant service. It has no sign-up, account-linking form, mailbox interface, or publicly accessible agent endpoint. Publishing these pages does not publish anyone's emails, documents, credentials, or assistant conversations.

Privacy and contact

Google data and selected task context may be processed by configured AI providers when needed to perform an authorized task. Read the privacy policy for information about access, use, storage, sharing, retention, and revocation.
